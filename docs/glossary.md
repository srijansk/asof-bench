# Glossary

The terms the documents use, each with the section of [PREREGISTRATION.md](../PREREGISTRATION.md) that defines it; where an entry and the registration seem to differ, the registration is the rule.

- **As of D**: as things stood at the end of the day D, in UTC. A repository's as-of commit for D is its last first-parent commit whose recorded time is at or before the end of D ([section 3](../PREREGISTRATION.md#3-recorded-time-and-as-of)).
- **S**: the snapshot date, 2026-10-27. Each repository is pinned at its last commit recorded by the end of S, and "at S" means at the end of that day ([section 2](../PREREGISTRATION.md#2-corpora-and-pins)).
- **Pin**: the commit each repository is read up to, listed under `pins` in data/v0.1/MANIFEST.json ([section 2](../PREREGISTRATION.md#2-corpora-and-pins)).
- **First-parent chain**: a repository's history from its root to the pin along each commit's first parent, with each merge read as its diff against its first parent ([section 3](../PREREGISTRATION.md#3-recorded-time-and-as-of)).
- **Recorded time**: when a change was written into the first-parent chain, not when it was decided: the larger of a commit's committer timestamp and its first parent's recorded time ([section 3](../PREREGISTRATION.md#3-recorded-time-and-as-of)).
- **Family**: one of the six kinds of question, F1 to F5 and CF ([section 7](../PREREGISTRATION.md#7-question-families)).
- **Stratum**: a sampled part of a family, such as F1-change, F1-current or F1-recent ([section 7](../PREREGISTRATION.md#7-question-families)).
- **Last offered status at S**: a proposal's status at S when a question offers that status, and otherwise the last status before S that a question offers, as for a proposal that is Stagnant at S ([section 7](../PREREGISTRATION.md#7-question-families)).
- **F1-change**: the F1 items whose status on the date differs from the proposal's last offered status at S. The headline is read on them, in arm H- ([sections 1](../PREREGISTRATION.md#1-the-question) and [7](../PREREGISTRATION.md#7-question-families)).
- **Arm**: one presentation of the corpora, each with its own stream. H+ keeps the headers as recorded; H- withholds the status, decision and relation header lines (the registration writes H−); CF is the counterfactual stream ([section 8](../PREREGISTRATION.md#8-arms-and-the-dated-revision-stream)).
- **Episode**: one proposal's change at one first-parent commit, with the commit's message ([section 8](../PREREGISTRATION.md#8-arms-and-the-dated-revision-stream)).
- **Stream**: an arm's file of episodes in recorded-time order, rebuilt locally from the clones by `asofbench stream` ([section 8](../PREREGISTRATION.md#8-arms-and-the-dated-revision-stream)).
- **Header-only episode**: in H-, the episode of a commit that changed only withheld header lines, kept with an empty diff and its message ([section 8](../PREREGISTRATION.md#8-arms-and-the-dated-revision-stream)).
- **Message cue**: a commit message kept in H- that names an item's gold status by its date; results are also reported apart by it ([section 12](../PREREGISTRATION.md#12-answers-and-scoring)).
- **Counterfactual (CF)**: the family and arm whose stream permutes the status headers of 30 proposals throughout their history and rewrites the messages of the commits that change them, so that an answer from what a model already knows about the real proposals is wrong ([sections 7](../PREREGISTRATION.md#7-question-families) and [10](../PREREGISTRATION.md#10-contamination-controls)).
- **Grep at D**: the version-control baseline: an agent with read-only shell tools over its arm's proposal files as of the end of the item's date and the log of the commits up to it, a reader of the repository and its history as they stood on the date ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Closed book**: the answering model given the question alone ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Long context**: the answering model given the question and every episode of the proposal the question names, in stream order, for F1, F2 and CF ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **The oracle**: the statements in effect at the item's date, read from the headers that define the gold; it is reported as a ceiling and never compared, because it wins by construction ([sections 1](../PREREGISTRATION.md#1-the-question) and [11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Answering, construction and embedding model**: the model that answers every question, the model that builds every store a language model builds (both claude-haiku-5-5 in the registered runs), and the one embedding model of every system that embeds text, voyage-4-nano ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Entrant and outside entry**: an entrant is a system that section 11 enters in v0.1; any other system is an outside entry, declared in an entries file, shown as `<id> (outside entry)` and never compared in the headline ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Entries file**: the JSON file, given to `asofbench score` with `--entries`, that names the arms and families each system is entered in ([section 12](../PREREGISTRATION.md#12-answers-and-scoring)).
- **Scored run**: any run of a system that answers a v0.1 item, whatever is done with its answers ([section 11](../PREREGISTRATION.md#11-systems-model-and-runs)).
- **Registered full run**: the scoring run whose headline makes claims: every item, the registered entries, all 14 comparisons with grep at D and each compared system answering every compared item in all three runs, the registered models and settings, and the registered build ([section 12](../PREREGISTRATION.md#12-answers-and-scoring)).
- **Claim and reading**: a comparison's verdict, above grep at D, below it or no detectable difference, is a claim in the registered full run and a reading in every other run ([section 12](../PREREGISTRATION.md#12-answers-and-scoring)).
- **Trivial-answer floor**: the share of a group's items whose gold is its most common value, the accuracy of always giving that answer; REPORT.md lists them ([section 12](../PREREGISTRATION.md#12-answers-and-scoring)).
- **M0, the cost pilot**: Graphiti ingests the episodes of 50 PEPs, and its measured tokens per character project the cost of every ingestion it needs against the budget, before any scored run ([section 13](../PREREGISTRATION.md#13-gates)).
- **graphiti-reduced**: Graphiti as entered when M0's projection exceeds the budget: it ingests the proposals the F1, F2 and CF questions name and as many others as fit, and is entered in F1, F2 and CF only ([section 13](../PREREGISTRATION.md#13-gates)).
- **M1, the pilot**: 180 items answered three times by six systems, whose gates decide whether the full runs go ahead ([section 13](../PREREGISTRATION.md#13-gates)).
- **The gates**: ceiling, loading, saturation and signal, applied in that order to the pilot's H- F1 results ([section 13](../PREREGISTRATION.md#13-gates)).
- **Registered pilot run**: the scoring run of exactly pilot.json's items, given with `--items`, with the registered models and settings on the registered build; only there do the gates decide ([section 13](../PREREGISTRATION.md#13-gates)).
- **Extraction track**: the measurement of what a system's store records, apart from its answers: an engine's store, built from the H- stream, is mapped to statements and edges and scored against the withheld headers, with 200 labelled mention pairs for relation precision ([section 9](../PREREGISTRATION.md#9-extraction-track-headers-withheld)).
- **Mention pair**: two proposals of one corpus, both present at S, where one's text mentions the other and no header relates them; the extraction track samples 200 of them ([section 9](../PREREGISTRATION.md#9-extraction-track-headers-withheld)).
- **Canary**: the string every data file carries, so that benchmark data can be kept out of training corpora ([section 10](../PREREGISTRATION.md#10-contamination-controls)).
