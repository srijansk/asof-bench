# Known limitations

[PREREGISTRATION.md](../PREREGISTRATION.md) registers every rule this page describes, and is the rule wherever the two seem to differ.

- **Recorded time and the pull merges of python/peps** ([section 3](../PREREGISTRATION.md#3-recorded-time-and-as-of)). Dates are recorded time, when a change was written into the repository's first-parent chain, not decided time. In python/peps, 14 first-parent merges named "Merge branch 'master' of github.com:python/peps", committed from 2016-06-26 to 2018-08-29 (2016-06-27 to 2018-08-29 in UTC), are pull merges: a committer's local branch is their first parent and the public branch their second, so the public changes they bring in are dated at the merge. Together they hide changes to 130 PEPs, over spans of up to 164 days between the first hidden change and its merge; the largest, 7914641, dates changes made from 2018-02-05 at 2018-07-20, so PEP 572 first appears as Accepted, with no Draft period. The EIP repository has 4 such merges, each hiding at most two days, and the ERC repository, which shares that early history, has the same 4, which touch no proposal file. No item is dated inside a span that hides a change to its own proposal, and no F4 or F5 date falls inside any such span, so no gold value depends on these merges. One F1 item is dated inside a span, one that does not hide its own proposal: F1-change-PEP-498-2016-06-23 inside a span of its own repository, b409f87's (2016-06-19 to 2016-06-27), which hides 13 other PEPs' changes; its gold does not read them. timelines.jsonl shows these proposals as the first-parent chain records them.
- **Empty ERC renames** (section 8). Each of the 366 ERCs that began in the EIP repository has, as its first ERC-repository episode, a rename whose diff is taken against its last kept EIP-repository text, and for 365 of them the diff is empty, because the text moved unchanged; the stubs and `Moved` markers that the move left in the EIP repository give no episode ([section 6](../PREREGISTRATION.md#6-eips-and-ercs-are-one-series)). So the move shows in a stream only as that rename and its commit message. The other 258 ERCs, created in the ERC repository, start with a create.
- **H- message cues** (sections 8 and 12). H- keeps commit messages, because they are prose that anyone reading the repository sees, and for 213 of the 503 H- F1 items a kept message names the gold status by D. An H- score therefore mixes reading the proposals' prose with reading the messages; `--message-cues` separates the two.
- **Header-only H- episodes** (sections 8 and 12). H- keeps, with an empty diff and its message, the episode of every commit that changed only withheld header lines: 1,875 of its episodes, 563 PEP and 1,312 Ethereum, all modify episodes, and 1,691 of them (476 PEP, 1,215 Ethereum) change a status line in H+ (counts that the corpus test `test_the_f1_header_only_flag_is_what_the_hminus_and_hplus_streams_show`, in tests/test_corpus_stream.py, recomputes from the two streams). Such an episode recorded after the item's date marks 213 of the 503 F1 items, among them 198 of the 300 H- F1-change items (71 PEP, 127 Ethereum), so on most headline items a system that reads the stream can see that a withheld header changed after D, and grep at D cannot. results.md reports every system's H- F1 accuracy apart by this flag, and the headline's first reading leaves the flagged items out, which leaves 79 PEP and 23 Ethereum items. On 23 items the exact McNemar test gives p below 0.05 only when at least 6 more of them are right for one system and wrong for the other than the reverse, and not always then: 6 to 0, 8 to 1, 9 to 1 and 10 to 2 pass, and 7 to 1 does not. Holm's step-down correction then multiplies the k-th smallest of the reading's 14 p values in a full run by 15 - k, and no adjusted p is smaller than one before it, so a result on these 23 items needs, with none the other way, 10 such items as the smallest p, 9 from the 3rd smallest on, 8 from the 9th, 7 from the 12th and 6 only as the 14th, with every comparison before it rejected too. So on Ethereum this reading can show only a large difference: at least 6 more of its 23 items one way than the other, 6 to 0 counting only as the last comparison Holm rejects, and 10 to 0 needed as the smallest p.
